# Alongway

### On Your Way. Here for Each Other.

**A Student-Powered Campus Delivery Platform at UNC-CH**

## Overview

Alongway is an unofficial student project exploring a route-based delivery network for the UNC-Chapel Hill community. 
Our goal is to create more social and economic value from students' everyday trips.

Every day, people travel between classrooms, libraries, dining locations, and residence halls. 
While some people are willing to take a small detour,
others have busy schedules filled with classes, research, and other responsibilities, 
making additional trips time-consuming.
Alongway aims to make better use of these existing trips by connecting people 
who need deliveries with students already heading in the same direction.

Students can post delivery requests or share their planned routes. 
By matching these requests with existing routes, 
the platform allows students to earn extra money or help others without making a separate trip.

Beyond convenience and financial rewards, 
Alongway also aims to create opportunities for meaningful social connections. 
Through small acts of help, students can meet people they might not otherwise 
interact with and build relationships within the campus community.

## Problem

Students often travel between dining locations,
classrooms, libraries, and residence halls along
similar routes. However, these trips are usually
made independently.

Alongway explores how existing campus routes can
become opportunities for convenient deliveries and
social interaction.

## Proposed Features

- Campus delivery requests
- User route sharing
- Route-based delivery matching
- Delivery acceptance and status tracking
- Optional financial and social incentives
- Student and faculty participation

## Initial Scope

The first prototype focuses on Fast food pickup
requests, using sample data and simulated interactions.
